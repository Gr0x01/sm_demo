## Selection Process (The Gap)

### What they have today

**Physical Design Studio**: 7350 Shoppes Dr, Suite 103, Viera FL 32940
- Director of Design: Nicole Neufeld
- SVP Sales & Marketing: Mary Mead
- Two 3-hour design appointments (Tue-Sat, 9am or 1pm):
  - Meeting 1 ("Heart of the Home"): exterior colors, cabinets, flooring, countertops, plumbing
  - Meeting 2 ("Finishing Touches"): ceiling plans, lighting, electrical, final authorization
- Monday open houses 11am-7pm (no appointment, preview only)
- Physical samples: flooring, countertops, hardware, fixtures, cabinets
- Kitchen vignettes on display spanning "various genres and tastes"
- Low-voltage rep meets buyers separately for tech specs

**Online Tools (already have some digital presence)**:
1. **"Express Yourself" Virtual Kitchen Designer** — web-based configurator. Change cabinet colors/styles, backsplash tile, flooring, countertops. Floor plans shown: Tamber, Claire, Audrey, Iris, Ella, Merryvale. Technology vendor unknown.
2. **Virtual Bath Designer** — similar web tool. Cabinet colors, shower tile height, combinations. Example styles: Highland, Audrey.
3. **Flex Options Tool** — interactive floorplan modifier. Add/remove bonus rooms, courtyards, lanais, summer kitchens. Shows real-time floorplan updates.
4. **Exterior Color Tool** — preview elevation styles and color combos.
5. **Personalized brochure generation** — buyers create a digital brochure of their selections to download/print/email to sales manager.

**Buyer journey**: Browse online (interactive maps, floorplans, virtual designers) -> create personalized brochure -> book appointment via "Ask Felicia" -> visit design studio with brochure in hand -> two 3-hour design meetings.

### The gap (where Finch fits)

Their virtual kitchen/bath designers are **static configurators** (swap materials in a template view), not visualization in the actual room. The "framed view" language suggests a fixed camera angle with swappable surfaces, not AI-generated room visualization.

Key gaps:
- Configurators show generic kitchen layouts, NOT the buyer's specific floorplan kitchen
- No visualization of how selections look together in the actual room context
- Two 3-hour appointments = 6 hours of design studio time per buyer (expensive)
- The brochure they generate is a selection list, not a visual preview
- Buyers see samples individually but still struggle to picture the full composition
- The "Express Yourself" tool covers kitchen/bath only. No living room, bedroom, or other room visualization.

**However**: They are MORE digitally advanced than most regional builders. They've already invested in online tools. This is a double-edged sword: they might feel "solved" on digital design, OR they might be the exact type of builder who sees the next step clearly.

## DM Strategy for Matt Sims

### Why Matt (not Robin or Nicole)

Robin Aldridge is Division President (already in Notion as "Passed"). Nicole Neufeld is Design Director (harder to reach, more invested in current process). Matt is:
- New in role, building relationships
- Sales-side (feels upgrade revenue directly)
- Active on LinkedIn (posts regularly)
- Consumer behavior thinker (will engage with the concept)
- Potential internal champion who surfaces it to Nicole/Mary/Robin

### Hook angles

1. **The courtyard homes angle**: He posts about courtyard living. These homes have outdoor/indoor flow. Buyers choosing between a standard lanai and a summer kitchen upgrade can't picture it from a price sheet. The courtyard IS the upgrade story.

2. **The configurator vs. room angle**: Viera already has virtual kitchen/bath designers. Matt knows those tools exist. The angle isn't "you need digital" (they already believe that). It's "your configurator shows a generic kitchen. What if buyers saw their selections in the Almeria II kitchen?"

3. **The consumer behavior angle**: His bio says he thinks about consumer behavior. The visualization lift data (20-40% more in option sales) is a consumer behavior finding, not a tech pitch. Lead with the behavioral insight.

4. **The $50K discount angle**: When you're discounting base price $50K to move homes, every dollar of upgrade revenue matters more. Visualization helps buyers spend on the things they actually want.

### Recommended DM approach

Don't pitch Finch. Don't mention tools. Talk about the behavioral pattern he'd find interesting as someone who thinks about consumer behavior. Reference Reeling Park specifically. Let him ask "how?"
