## Design Center / Selection Process

### Physical design center
- Location: 7020 N Van Ness Blvd, Suite 103, Fresno, CA 93711
- **Appointment only**
- Dedicated Design Consultant guides each buyer

### Selection categories
Flooring, cabinetry, countertops, fixtures, window coverings, tile, wiring/media/technology

### Named brands
Frigidaire, Pfister, Kohler, Daltile, Whirlpool, Sherwin-Williams, Mohawk

### Appointment structure (varies by collection)
- **Boulevard & Poppy** (entry-level): Single appointment with "professionally curated packages that simplify decision-making"
- **Savanna & Ivy** (move-up): Two appointments
  - Appointment 1: Behind-the-wall items (wiring, media, tech) + long-lead-time materials
  - Appointment 2: The "fun" details — tile, flooring, countertops, etc.

### Key quote from their blog
"Everything is displayed in a way that helps you visualize real combinations, not just pick from a list."

### Buyer advice on their site
"Walk the models prior to your appointment noting options of interest" and bring "inspiration photos, fabrics or information that illustrates your personal vision."

### Why McCaffrey is a strong fit
1. **No visualization tech at all** — purely physical design center, appointment only
2. **Clear upgrade gap**: Entry homes (Boulevard/Poppy) use "curated packages" — buyers can't see what individual upgrades look like. Move-up homes (Savanna/Ivy) have full selection but buyers must "walk the models" to imagine combinations.
3. **Their own blog says** "not just pick from a list" as a selling point — they know the list problem exists
4. **24+ floor plans across 6 neighborhoods** — enough scale to justify a tool
5. **Family builder that prides itself on buyer experience** — they'd care about this
6. **Two-builder opportunity** via De Young Properties cousin relationship
7. **Greg is mutual connection to Karen** — warm intro possible
