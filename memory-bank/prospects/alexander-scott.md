## Design Center / Selections Process

### What they have today
- **No dedicated design center found.** No "design center" or "design studio" page on website.
- **No visualization tools detected.** No Envision, Roomored, or any digital tool on the website. No virtual design tool. No online configurator.
- **Matterport 3D tours**: They use VantagePoint3D for Matterport walkthroughs of model homes (Langston model at Swann's Bridge). This shows they value visual selling but it's for the model home, not for selections.
- **"Personalized design selections"** — The Bradford blog post mentions homes "early enough in construction that buyers can make personalized design selections." This is their language for the upgrade process.
- **Physical samples only** — No evidence of any digital tool. Likely PDF spec sheets or in-person showings at the model home.
- **Tech level**: Very low. WordPress/Divi website. No app, no portal, no online design tool.

### What Warren knows from Providence Group
- TPG has a 5,000 sq ft Design Studio with professional interior designers
- 3-5 hour design consultations, scheduled by a Design Coordinator
- Full selections: flooring, tile, cabinetry, countertops, fixtures, hardware
- This is the model Warren has operated in for 20 years. He knows what a mature design center looks like.

### The Gap
Warren knows what a full design center operation should look like, but Alexander Scott Homes is too new and too small to build one. They're a 30-60 home/year builder ramping toward 100+. A 5,000 sq ft Design Studio isn't in the budget yet. That's exactly where Finch fits — give buyers the visualization experience without the physical infrastructure.
