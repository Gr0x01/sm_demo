## Design Center / Design Studio

ICI calls it the **"Design Studio"** — a one-stop "Dream Shop."

### Two-Visit Process
1. **Pre-contract visit** (~1 hour): Top-line selections so sales agents can estimate costs for the contract.
2. **Post-contract visit** (full day): Finalize everything — all interior and exterior color and material selections.

### What's Inside
Well-lit studio stocked with kitchen and bathroom mockups, countertop/hardware/color samples, flooring, paint colors. Kitchen and bathroom cabinetry mock-ups on display.

### Staff
Professional interior decorators (not just "design consultants") who advise on taste, needs, and budget.

### Locations
- **Daytona Beach**: 2379 Beville Rd, Daytona Beach, FL 32119
- **Jacksonville**: 14785 Old St. Augustine Rd, Ste 3, Jacksonville, FL 32258

### Key Quote (from their website)
> "Do your best homework BEFORE your design center appointment. Changes made after purchase orders have been released and/or construction has begun are costly and will delay the construction process."

### Selection Categories (from research)
Flooring, countertops, cabinets, faucets, paint colors, hardware, electrical outlet placement, light switches, appliances, smart home technology, outdoor living options.

### Custom Options (30+)
Custom kitchen islands, open floor plans, specialized rooms (gym, playroom, game room, music room), smart home tech, epoxy garage floors, pet-washing stations, mudrooms, outdoor living (summer kitchens, fire pits, outdoor fireplaces).
