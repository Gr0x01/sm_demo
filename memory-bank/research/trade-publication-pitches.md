# Trade Publication Pitch Targets

Research piece: **"The Hidden Revenue Line"** — withfin.ch/research/hidden-revenue-line
Analysis of SEC filings showing upgrade/options revenue across 14+ public builders. Key data points: Toll Brothers $203K/home (20.8% of ASP), PulteGroup $104K/home, Taylor Morrison $236K/home. Most builders don't disclose or benchmark this line.

## PITCH STRATEGY

### Ordering the outreach

1. **BDMag (Nick Slevin)** — Smaller pub, easier placement, production builder focus. Most likely to actually run a contributed piece.
2. **Pro Builder (Rich Binsacca)** — Pitch as data source for their editorial team. Right audience, but they write their own content.
3. **Builder Magazine (Jennifer Lash)** — Same as Pro Builder. Pitch as data source, not contributed article.
4. **JBREC (Jenni Nichols / John Burns LinkedIn)** — Not a pitch, a relationship. Plant the seed.
5. **Market Proof Marketing (Kevin Oakley)** — Podcast guest pitch. Different format, no exclusivity conflict.
6. **HousingWire (Tracey Velt)** — Pitch as data source rather than contributed article (exclusivity issue).
7. **Construction Dive** — Write a fresh opinion piece if capacity allows.
8. **NAHB** — Long shot for direct placement, but worth the email.

### The exclusivity problem

The full analysis is already live at withfin.ch/research/hidden-revenue-line. This means:
- **Pro Builder, Builder**: Ad-supported media companies. Pitch as data source for their own editorial, not a contributed article. If they cite the research in their coverage, that's the win.
- **BDMag**: Smaller, more accessible. Best shot at an actual contributed piece or feature.
- **HousingWire, Construction Dive**: Require exclusive content. Write derivative pieces with different angles if pursuing these.
- **JBREC, NAHB**: Citation/reference play — no exclusivity concern.
- **Podcasts**: No exclusivity concern. The research IS the talking point.

### What to include in every pitch

1. The hook number: "$104K to $236K per home in upgrade revenue — from SEC filings"
2. The gap: "Most builders don't disclose or benchmark this line"
3. The link: withfin.ch/research/hidden-revenue-line
4. The ask: Clear and specific (contributed piece, data source, podcast guest)
5. The bio: Rashaad, founder of Finch. Keep it one sentence. The research stands on its own.

### What NOT to do

- Don't pitch Finch the product. Pitch the research. Let them find the product.
- Don't send a wall of text. 3-5 sentences max for initial outreach.
- Don't mass-email. Each pitch is personalized to the publication's editorial focus.
- Don't follow up more than twice. If they're not interested, move on.
