# National Builder Prospects

Research date: 2026-03-09. Consolidated from national-builder-prospects.md and national-builders-tier2-3.md.

## Key Finding: Envision Options (by Zonda) is the Incumbent

Multiple builders use **Envision Options** (edc2.envisionoptions.com), a Zonda/NewHomeSource configurator/wishlist tool. It organizes the selection process but does NOT provide visual rendering or AI visualization. This is Finch's opening: Envision tells you what you can pick. Finch shows you what it looks like.

Builders on Envision: Taylor Morrison, Tri Pointe Homes, KB Home, M/I Homes.

## Competitive Intelligence

- **Envision Options (Zonda)** — dominant configurator. Selection/wishlist, NOT visualizer. Finch is additive.
- **Roomored (Interior Logic Group)** — only real visualization competitor. 3D rendering on floorplans. Requires 3D modeling (expensive/slow setup vs Finch's photo-based approach). Used by Shea Homes.
- **Matterport** — virtual tours of physical studios, not interactive selection visualization.
- **Kitchen 360 (Toll Brothers)** — proprietary VR app, limited deployment, requires headset.
- **No builder on this list uses AI-generated visualization.**
