## 5. NAHB / HBA CHANNELS

### NAHB Blog and Newsletters
- **Accepts outside content?**: NO — staff-written. The blog and Eye on Housing are produced by NAHB's economics team (Robert Dietz et al.) and communications staff.
- **What's possible**: NAHB does feature member content in newsletters like Monday Morning Briefing if it's newsworthy. Getting the research picked up by a trade pub first makes it more likely NAHB will link to it.
- **Contact**: communications@nahb.org for general inquiries.

### Local HBA Newsletters
- **Accepts outside content?**: VARIES. Many local HBAs have newsletters and accept member-contributed content. Some examples:
  - Houston Builder Magazine (GHBA) — accepts member submissions for awards, achievements, and industry content.
  - Most local HBAs have a newsletter editor who's always looking for content.
- **Strategy**: Join the local HBA in your target markets (Birmingham, Atlanta). Offer to present the research at a monthly meeting. The newsletter write-up follows naturally.
- **Best use**: Not for national distribution. For regional credibility when you're trying to land builders in that specific market.
