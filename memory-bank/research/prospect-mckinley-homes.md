## Selection Process

- **Design Preview Session**: Complimentary pre-appointment session for buyers to preview personalization options and pricing. This is the key hook -- they already know buyers need to see options before the real appointment.
- **Pre-Construction Meeting**: Required for all homebuyers. Reviews Design Selections, Site Plans, Blueprints with Construction Manager at the home site.
- **Design appointments**: In-person with design team member, walk through all available finishes.
- **No online design center or visualization tool** found on their website.
- **"The McKinley Difference"**: Standard inclusions positioned as upgrades (soft-close cabinets, SPC flooring, designer backsplash, 48" fireplace).
