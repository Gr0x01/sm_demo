## TIER 1 — Best Fit (Private, Right Volume, Low Tech, Design Center)

### 1. Classic Homes

- **HQ**: Colorado Springs, CO
- **Volume**: ~500 homes/yr (500+ delivered in 2022)
- **Tech Level**: Low-Medium
- **Design Center**: Yes — "Design Studio" at 2754 North Gate Blvd, Colorado Springs. Virtual appointments via GoToMeeting/FaceTime for initial consultation, then in-person selection. Online Design Studio Prep tool walks buyers through selections. No AI visualization or interactive configurator.
- **Ownership**: Private (principals: Jeff Smith Chairman, Doug Stimple CEO, George Lentz CFO, Dan Winter EVP Marketing)
- **Primary Contact**: Dan Winter — Executive VP of Marketing (co-owner)
- **Secondary Contact**: Doug Stimple — CEO
- **Company Phone**: (719) 592-9333
- **Design Studio Phone**: (719) 785-0123
- **Website**: classichomes.com
- **Why Finch**: Largest builder in El Paso County, 35+ years. Named "Best Home Builder" 16 consecutive years. Their Design Studio Prep is a glorified checklist, not a visualizer. 500+ homes/yr with a semi-custom model means significant upgrade revenue at stake. Dan Winter as EVP Marketing and co-owner is the ideal decision-maker — he owns both the brand and the sales experience. Virtual-first design appointments = already tech-willing but lacking visual tools.
- **Existing Tools**: Design Studio Prep (online checklist), GoToMeeting for virtual appointments. No visualization.
- **Builder 100**: Revenue $100M-$500M range, 132 employees

## REGIONAL DENSITY OPPORTUNITIES

### Colorado Springs, CO (3 builders on this list)
Classic Homes + Challenger Homes + Vantage Homes all build in the same metro. Land one, use proximity to land the others. CO Springs is a strong first west coast market for Finch.

### Utah (3 builders on this list)
Holmes Homes (Salt Lake area) + Fieldstone Homes (Draper/multi-county) + Ence Homes (St. George). Holmes + Fieldstone overlap in the Wasatch Front. Land one, pitch the other.

### Boise, ID (1 builder + 1 secondary)
CBH Homes dominates Boise. Land CBH, then approach Brighton Homes and smaller Boise builders from a position of proof.

### Pacific Northwest (2 builders)
New Tradition Homes (Vancouver, WA / Portland metro) + Pahlisch Homes (Bend / Central OR + expanding). Different sub-markets but same regional conference circuit.
