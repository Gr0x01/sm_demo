### How it works
- Buyers preview catalogs first
- Then get a **4-hour individual design appointment** with a designer

## What Stood Out

### The opportunity
1. **4-hour in-person design appointments with zero digital visualization.** Buyers preview catalogs, then sit with a designer for 4 hours picking finishes. No way to see those selections in the actual room before committing. At $750K-$1.4M price points, these buyers would absolutely engage with room visualization.

2. **Luxury price points = higher upgrade potential.** At $515K-$1.4M base prices, the upgrade gap is significant. Luxury buyers expect to personalize extensively. The "whole gamut from A to Z" selection process means lots of upgrade decisions happening in one sitting.

3. **Semi-custom model = many configurable options.** They explicitly call their homes "semi-custom" with "flexible options for design." Every floor plan has multiple layout and finish variations. This creates complexity that visualization helps simplify.

4. **PureStyle is the design authority but not a tech team.** Charlene Neal's team designs beautiful model homes. But there's no tool helping buyers bridge the gap between model home inspiration and their own selection choices. Model homes show one curated version, not the buyer's version.

5. **Multiple boutique communities = manageable scope.** 4-5 active communities with 4-9 floor plans each. Not trying to cover 50 communities. Right size for a Finch rollout.

### Carlos Puente as target
- **PulteGroup background** means he's seen what big builders do with tech. Pulte has design center visualization tools.
- **Sales-driven career** (Sales Associate of the Year) — he understands upgrade revenue.
- **Division President with P&L ownership** — can make buying decisions for Neal Signature.
- **MBA + IT master's** — not tech-averse.
- **$70M+ division** — enough scale for ROI but small enough to not need enterprise procurement.

### Potential objections
- They have a strong in-person design process with Charlene Neal's team — "we don't need a screen, we have a Design Gallery"
- PureStyle's model homes are the visualization — beautiful, curated, award-winning
- Luxury buyers may expect the white-glove in-person experience over digital tools
- They have Matterport tours already, might feel "we have digital covered"
