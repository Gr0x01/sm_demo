## Outreach Angle
**DO NOT reference the ECI case study or the +20% stat on Signature's own demo page.** They already know their own results. We cite that stat on OTHER builders' pages.

This is Rashaad's home market (Birmingham). The angle is:
- Finch extends the Design Studio to every buyer's phone, not replaces it
- They already invested in ECI Insearch for floor plans and site maps — Finch adds photorealistic room visualization on top of that
- Their Design Studio is inside model homes; Finch lets buyers explore finishes when they're not at the model
- At their price point ($400K-$800K), upgrade revenue is a meaningful line item
- Employee-owned company = everyone benefits from higher margins

**Key framing**: "You've already got the interactive floor plans. This is the room-level piece — what their kitchen actually looks like with the selections they're considering."
