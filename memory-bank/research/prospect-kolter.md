# Kolter Homes — Prospect Research Brief

## Company
- **Parent**: The Kolter Group (private, Palm Beach FL)
- **Brand**: Kolter Homes (homebuilding division)
- **Volume**: ~1,500 closings/yr, $1.275B revenue (2024), Builder Magazine #48
- **Markets**: FL (South FL, Port St. Lucie, Orlando, Panhandle, Sarasota/Bradenton), GA (Atlanta/Newnan), NC (Charlotte), SC (Charleston), AL (Gulf Coast)
- **Communities**: 23+ active across 9 markets
- **Price points**: $300K (active adult entry) to $2M+ (Artistry Palm Beach luxury)
- **Focus**: Heavy active adult 55+ via Cresswind brand. Also non-age-restricted (Artistry, Mosaic)

## Design Studio
- They call it "Design Studio" (not design center)
- **Ultimate Design Studio**: 5,000 sq ft at Cresswind at Spring Haven (Newnan, GA). Three working kitchens, two bathroom vignettes, private selection offices. Opened Aug 2024.
- **Second studio**: Esprit at Avenir (Palm Beach Gardens). Opened Feb 2026.
- **Online**: Basic "Kitchen Finish Visualizer" (static swatch tool). No Envision, Roomored, or ECI.
- **Gap**: 23+ communities, 2 physical studios. Most buyers make selections without the immersive experience.
- **Their language**: "Design Studio," "personalize," "design professionals," "complimentary consultation," "curated selections"
