## Research Findings (for reference when writing more)

The 7 real reasons builders haven't adopted visualization tools:

| # | Barrier | Finch answer |
|---|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Cost: months setup, six figures, hidden pricing | $500/mo, go live in days |
| 2 | Trust deficit: burned by unreliable software | Demo on THEIR plan, see it working |
| 3 | "Good enough" status quo: broken but generates margin | Show the margin they're leaving |
| 4 | No IT staff: 71% don't feel digital tools are relevant | We do the setup, no tech team needed |
| 5 | Vendor lock-in: tools tied to ILG/Hyphen/ECI ecosystems | Standalone, no ERP required |
| 6 | Staffing: 58% turnover, can't train on new tools | Buyer-facing, no staff training |
| 7 | Bad viz worse than none: has to be photorealistic | AI-generated from actual room photos |

**Useful angles per barrier:**
- Capacity leads (like Tr): barrier #6 — same team, more output
- Premium leads (like Chad): barrier #3 — the gap between product and process
- Growth operators (like Kellie): barrier #1 — this used to be expensive, now it's not
- Family/legacy builders (like Troy): barrier #3 + #7 — the product evolved, the process didn't
- Military/remote clients: barrier #7 — can't visit, need to see it
- Multi-market builders: barrier #4 — consistent experience without IT staff per market
- Builders with portals/Matterport: barrier #5 — you already invest in digital, this is the missing piece
