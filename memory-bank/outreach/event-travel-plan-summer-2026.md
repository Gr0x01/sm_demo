## SUMMARY: The Big Wins

The travel schedule lines up with two **major** builder conferences and several promising local association events:

## LEG 1: SEATTLE AREA (May 23-25)

### Short window — focus on local HBA connections

#### MBAKS (Master Builders Association of King and Snohomish Counties)
- **What**: The nation's oldest and largest local HBA. 300+ events per year. Monthly builder council breakfasts, networking mixers, and educational programs.
- **Recurring events to look for in May**:
  - **King County Builders Council Breakfast** — Monthly, typically a weekday morning at 7:30 AM. Location rotates (often Bellevue area). Builder-focused, covers local housing policy and regulatory issues.
  - **Seattle Builders Council Breakfast** — Similar format, Seattle-focused.
  - **Snohomish County events** — Everett area, typically at Shawn O'Donnell's American Grill & Irish Pub.
  - **General Membership Networking** — Monthly, typically 4:00-6:00 PM at MBAKS HQ in Bellevue.
- **How to check**: Visit [my.mbaks.com/eBizUI/events/event-catalog](https://my.mbaks.com/eBizUI/events/event-catalog) or call 425-451-7920.
- **Membership**: Events are generally members-only. Guest attendance at some events may be possible — call and ask. Annual membership for associate members (vendors/suppliers) is available.
- **Why it matters**: Washington State has major production builders (Lennar, DR Horton, Toll Brothers active in the Puget Sound market). The council breakfasts are small enough to actually meet people.
- **Website**: [mbaks.com/events-and-programs](https://www.mbaks.com/events-and-programs)

#### BIAW (Building Industry Association of Washington)
- **What**: Statewide association, umbrella org for 14 local HBAs including MBAKS. Holds three board meetings per year (winter, summer, fall).
- **Summer board meeting**: Date and location TBD for 2026. Historically these rotate around the state.
- **How to check**: [biaw.com/biaw-board-meeting](https://www.biaw.com/biaw-board-meeting)
- **Why it matters**: Statewide networking with builders from across Washington, not just Seattle.

#### Master Builders Association of Pierce County (Tacoma)
- **What**: 800+ members in Pierce County. Separate from MBAKS.
- **Events**: Likely has monthly meetings. Worth checking if May 23-25 overlaps.
- **Website**: [masterbuilderspierce.com](https://masterbuilderspierce.com/)

#### Olympia Master Builders
- **What**: Thurston County HBA. Hosts annual BIG Home & Remodel Show (April — just missed it) and Tour of Homes (September).
- **Website**: [omb.org/events/](https://omb.org/events/)

### Seattle Strategy
Your window is only 3 days and hits a weekend (Fri-Sun). Most HBA events happen on weekday mornings/afternoons. **Action: Call MBAKS the week of May 11 and ask what's on the calendar for the week of May 23.** Even if nothing formal is scheduled, ask if there's a builder council breakfast or mixer that week. Mention you're a vendor with an AI visualization product for production builders and you're in town briefly — they may be able to connect you directly.

## LEG 2: SACRAMENTO AREA (May 25 - June 10)

### Best window for local HBA engagement

#### North State Building Industry Association (North State BIA)
- **What**: Sacramento region's HBA. 500+ members including major production builders (Lennar, Taylor Morrison, KB Home all active — Lennar's Michelle Velky and Taylor Morrison's Ryan Hatheway are on the 2026 board).
- **Recurring events to look for in May/June**:
  - **Builder/Developer Council Meeting** — Monthly, typically 8:30-9:30 AM. Discusses regional building issues.
  - **Placer Builder/Developer Council** — Similar format for Placer County builders.
  - **Breakfast Blend** — Educational breakfast series, held at the Hilton Arden West in Sacramento. Spring and Fall editions.
  - **BRIDGE** — Young professionals group. Regular meetings and community service events.
  - **Sales & Marketing Council** — Monthly meetings focused on new home sales and marketing.
  - **Chairman's Circle Partners** — Running through June 1, 2026 (membership program).
- **Annual events**: Golf tournament (date TBD), Crab Feed (typically winter/spring), Awards events.
- **How to check**: [northstatebia.org/events/](https://www.northstatebia.org/events/) or call 916-677-5717.
- **Why it matters**: Sacramento is one of the largest new home markets in California. Taylor Morrison, Lennar, KB Home, and several mid-size regional builders all operate here. The Sales & Marketing Council meetings would be the ideal place to meet design center managers and VPs of Sales.
- **Membership**: Most events require BIA membership. Associate membership is available for vendors.

#### BIA Bay Area
- **What**: Building Industry Association of the Bay Area. 400+ members. Covers Bay Area builders.
- **Recurring events**: Three P.A.S.S. (Purchasing Agents, Subcontractors and Suppliers) luncheons per year, annual Golf Tournament, Spring Builder Panel Breakfast, MAME Awards (Sales & Marketing Council), regional committee meetings.
- **How to check**: [biabayarea.org/events](https://www.biabayarea.org/events)
- **Why it matters**: Bay Area builders (Toll Brothers, Taylor Morrison, Shea Homes, Meritage) are high-end production builders who spend more on design centers. A P.A.S.S. luncheon or builder panel breakfast would be ideal.

#### CBIA (California Building Industry Association)
- **What**: Statewide association. Hosts PCBC. May have a governance meeting or smaller event in the May/June window.
- **Contact**: Beth Harris at (916) 340-3313 or bharris@cbia.org
- **Website**: [cbia.org/events/](https://cbia.org/events/)

### Sacramento Strategy
You have two full weeks here. **Action: Contact North State BIA by May 10 to ask about any events the last week of May or first week of June.** Specifically ask about the Sales & Marketing Council meeting — that's where design center managers and new home sales VPs gather. Also ask about the Builder/Developer Council meetings. If you can attend even one breakfast meeting, you'll be in a room with exactly the right people. Consider joining as an associate member if it unlocks event access (typically $300-500/year).

## LEG 3: SAN ANTONIO AREA (June 15 - July ~2026)

### The jackpot leg — Sunbelt Builders Show is here

#### *** SUNBELT BUILDERS SHOW 2026 ***
- **Dates**: July 22-23, 2026 (Trade show). TAB summer meetings run July 21-24.
- **Location**: Henry B. Gonzalez Convention Center, San Antonio, TX
- **Registration**:
  - **FREE** for builders/developers/remodelers/architects/designers during May (advance pricing)
  - **$100** June 1 - July 27
  - **$150** on-site
- **What it is**: The Texas Association of Builders' annual trade show. 200+ exhibitors. Education sessions, keynotes, networking. Topics include housing trends, technology for builders, AI in marketing, vendor relationships, labor solutions.
- **Associated events**:
  - **Big Time on the Riverwalk Fundraiser** — July 22, evening. Networking event.
  - **Star Awards Dinner & Ceremony** — July 23, Grand Hyatt San Antonio. Texas' only statewide tribute to home building excellence.
- **Who attends**: Texas builders from across the state. San Antonio, Austin, Houston, Dallas/Fort Worth markets all represented.
- **Registration**: [sunbeltbuildersshow.com/registration/](https://www.sunbeltbuildersshow.com/registration/)
- **Website**: [sunbeltbuildersshow.com](https://www.sunbeltbuildersshow.com/)
- **Why this is the #1 event on this list**: It's in your city, during your window, with free registration (if you sign up in May), and it's specifically for residential home builders. The education sessions on builder technology and AI in marketing are natural conversation starters. The exhibit hall lets you walk the floor and see what builders are using now. The Riverwalk event is casual networking. **Register in May to get the free builder pass.**

#### Greater San Antonio Builders Association (GSABA)
- **What**: Local HBA. Active with monthly events.
- **Recurring events**:
  - **Membership Mixers** — Monthly networking events at rotating venues.
  - **Tech Breakfast Club** — Technology-focused morning events (had one March 19).
  - **Crawfish Boil** — Annual networking event.
  - **Young Professionals Leadership Council (YPLC)** — Regular events.
- **Major 2026 events**:
  - **Spring Tour of Homes** — May 16-25, 2026. 11 communities, 55+ homes across San Antonio. You'll miss this but it shows how active the local builder scene is.
  - **Parade of Homes** — August 29 - September 7, 2026. Too late for your window.
  - **Summit Awards** — August 6, 2026. Might overlap depending on your departure.
- **How to check**: [sabuilders.com](https://www.sabuilders.com/) or call (210) 696-3800.
- **Membership**: Associate membership available. Many events open to members only.
- **Why it matters**: San Antonio is a hot market. Builders like David Weekley, Chesmar, Perry Homes, Pulte, and multiple regional builders operate here. GSABA events are where they hang out informally.

#### Texas Association of Builders (TAB)
- **What**: Statewide builder association. Umbrella for all Texas HBAs.
- **2026 calendar highlights during your window**:
  - Sunbelt Builders Show (see above) is the main event.
  - TAB Summer Committee & Board Meetings run alongside the show (July 21-24).
- **Website**: [texasbuilders.org](https://www.texasbuilders.org/)

### San Antonio Strategy
**Register for Sunbelt Builders Show in May (free).** This is a can't-miss event. You and your wife can both work the floor. The education sessions give you conversation starters ("Did you see that session on AI in marketing? That's exactly what we built"). The Riverwalk event is the casual networking sweet spot. Before the show, contact GSABA about any membership mixers or Tech Breakfast events in June/July — those smaller events let you build relationships before the big show. If you meet someone interesting at a mixer, you can invite them to come see your demo at Sunbelt.
